# SpongeBob Movie: August 2004 PS2 prototype and original Xbox XDK trace

Date: 2026-10-22. This is research into the 2004 Heavy Iron console game, not Battle for Bikini Bottom or other SpongeBob titles.

## Concrete PS2 provenance

- Title: **The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie Game — Wata Certified Prototype [Test Build, 8/31/2004]**, PS2 THQ 2004 USA.
- Collection: **The Harvey Collection**.
- Auction: **Heritage Auctions 44210**, lot **79058**.
- Sale date: **September 7, 2023**.
- **Realized price: $1,440** according to Heritage's archived auction result list.
- Original item: https://comics.ha.com/itm/video-games/other/the-spongebob-squarepants-movie-game-wata-certified-prototype-test-build-8-31-2004-the-harvey-collection-ps2-thq-2004-usa/a/44210-79058.s
- Verified auction index: https://comics.ha.com/c/search/results.zx?archive_state=5327&auction_name=44210&auction_year=2023&dept=1938&ic=Items-OpenAuctions-Open-BrowseTitle-071713&mode=archive&sold_status=1526~1524

The sale establishes that a labeled physical prototype was publicly auctioned; it does **not** establish whether it contained a developer menu, that a disc image was dumped, or who bought it. The sale was not itself a preservation release.

## Xbox search result

Searched for the exact Movie title combined with `Xbox`, `XDK`, `debug`, `prototype`, `review code`, and Xbox preservation indexes, plus Archive.org and old forum sources.

**No verified public original Xbox prototype or XDK Movie build identified in this search pass.** Hidden Palace's broad prototype index contains many Xbox builds, but title-matched Movie entries visible from the index concern GameCube and GBA, not original Xbox.

- Hidden Palace prototype index: https://hiddenpalace.org/Prototypes_by_system
